Tools & Resources

A curated list of trusted platforms, tools, and educational sites to continue your crypto journey safely.

Educational Resources

CoinGecko Learn

Excellent, easy-to-read articles on specific crypto topics. Great for beginners.

Investopedia Crypto

Traditional finance site with rigorous, well-cited crypto definitions and guides.

Bitcoin.org

The original hub for learning how Bitcoin specifically works, in plain English.

Trusted Wallets

Ledger

Industry-standard cold (hardware) wallet. Best for securing large amounts long-term.

Trezor

Open-source alternative cold wallet with a strong security track record.

MetaMask

The most popular hot wallet for Ethereum and EVM-compatible networks.

Phantom

The premier hot wallet for the Solana network.

Reputable Exchanges

Coinbase

Heavily regulated US exchange. Great for beginners — simple interface, strong security.

Kraken

Security-focused exchange with a long track record and transparent practices.

Binance

Largest global exchange by volume. Note: not available for US residents.

Data & Blockchain Explorers

CoinGecko

Track prices, market cap, and find official project links. A good source of truth.

Etherscan

Blockchain explorer to track and verify any transaction on the Ethereum network.

DefiLlama

Track the total value locked in DeFi protocols to gauge their popularity and health.
Safety Note

Always verify URLs independently before entering sensitive information. Never search for these sites via search ads — type the URL directly or use a trusted bookmark. Scammers create convincing fake versions of popular crypto platforms.
